Questions

Does it need installing?

No. Pirate2000 is a single EXE that runs on its own. Delete the file and it’s gone.

Which systems does it run on?

Windows.

Where do my files go?

Into your Downloads folder, unless you choose another one with Browse… in the app.

A download failed. What now?

Read the message under the progress bar – it says what happened in plain words. Technical details are saved in a log file called Pirate2000_bugs in your Downloads folder.

Is anything I download my responsibility?

Yes. Only download content you have the right to save.
